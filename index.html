How to Take This Assessment

Below you will see a series of short phrases describing people’s behaviors and feelings. Please rate how accurately each statement describes you as you are now, not as you wish to be in the future.

For each statement, select the option that best fits:

  • Strongly Disagree: This is definitely not me.
  • Disagree: This is mostly not me.
  • Neutral: I am undecided, or this applies to me equally in both directions.
  • Agree: This is mostly me.
  • Strongly Agree: This is definitely me.

Tips for Accurate Results:

  • Be Honest: The test only works if you are truthful about your flaws and strengths.
  • Go with Your Gut: Don't overthink it. Your first reaction is usually the most accurate.
  • Focus on the Baseline: If your answer changes based on specific environments, relationships, or situations, go with the answer that applies to a neutral environment with a random person in a generic situation.
  • Answer Every Question: Do not skip any statements, even if you aren't completely sure. You may think some items are repeated or irrelevant, just answer them anyway and don't be a smartass I'm the psychologist here ok.
